## Concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: art direction target for an actual isometric 3D browser samurai combat game, ONISOLO.
Generate one widescreen 16:9 image that looks like a gorgeous real-time gameplay screenshot, without UI. Close orthographic isometric camera, 45-degree azimuth, about 38 degrees elevation, showing two sides and tops of the architecture. A rain-soaked Japanese shrine courtyard enclosed by layered mossy stone walls and aged charcoal-timber temple buildings. Dense crafted architectural edges frame an open, readable fighting floor: individually laid uneven warm-gray stone pavers, fine dark seams, small broken corners, wet cool blue-green puddles with restrained reflections, drainage channels, moss patches. Rear-center shrine has layered dark teal ceramic roof tiles, warm amber paper lanterns, wood lattices, a subdued vermilion entry and stone steps. Side architecture has weathered beams, copper edges, hanging ivy, clustered small pines and bamboo, a few barrels and crates. Thick detailed walls, rooftops, and props create a rich miniature diorama. One small clearly readable ivory-and-russet samurai with a katana stands near the middle, a few differently shaped enemies at the edges. Fine atmospheric rain, soft sun shafts from upper left, hazy cool background, warm lantern pools, convincing soft contact shadows. Restrained warm limestone, charcoal timber, desaturated teal, moss olive, amber; retain full material color. Handcrafted faceted beveled 3D geometry, sophisticated small-scale detail; no giant voxel bricks. This must be achievable in a well-optimized Three.js scene, so favor geometry, instancing and painted material detail over photoreal complexity. The courtyard fills the frame edge-to-edge, no floating island, no black void, no giant title or text, no interface, no letterbox, no film grain. Calm, cinematic, tactile, atmospheric; strong readable combat silhouettes.

## Paving albedo

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tileable diffuse albedo texture for a real-time 3D game's wet shrine courtyard floor. Square texture, orthographic perfectly top-down flat surface, no perspective. Dense individually hand-laid worn Japanese gray flagstones, small irregular rectangular slate and warm limestone paving stones in staggered rows, muted gray-olive and warm desaturated stone, narrow dark damp joints, tiny dark moss only in some cracks, chipped rounded edges, fine pitted mineral detail, small restrained patches of cool damp discoloration. Roughly 10 to 14 stones across the image. This is a tiling PBR base-color map: completely even soft diffuse illumination, absolutely no cast shadows, no directional lighting, no bright specular highlights, no actual reflective puddles, no gradients, no vignette, no objects, no leaves, no text. All four edges wrap naturally to the opposite edge. Rich restrained painterly realism matching a high-end miniature isometric rainy temple, not noisy rubble. Stone colors medium gray rather than dark black so in-engine lighting can shade the surface.
